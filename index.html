Bird Farm guide

Everything players and investors need to know before they play.

Earn BIRD with Bird Farm

Bird Farm is a farming game on the Supra chain. Anyone can play for free. Players who buy gems with BIRD join the Nest and farm for BIRD profit.

🌾 Free farm

Start with 💎 1 and 6 of 16 plots open. Plant, harvest, sell in the Barn, level up with XP and unlock new seeds. Free-farm gems stay in the game.

🪺 The Nest

For Early Birds, wallets that bought 💎 gems with BIRD. Your garden plants with paid gems and every crop pays profit in BIRD.

📜 Quests & Top

Finish quests for small gem rewards and climb the leaderboard with your XP.

🐦 BIRD token

The game token on Supra. Trade it on the floor or on WARs DEX, and track it in the Bird Screener.

How it works

1

Pick a seed

Choose a seed at the bottom of the farm, then tap an empty plot to plant it.

2

Wait and harvest

When the crop is ready, tap it. Harvested crops go to the Barn.

3

Sell for gems

Sell crops in the Barn for 💎 gems. Gems buy seeds, plots, Super Soil and Helper Birds.

4

Level up

XP unlocks new seeds and puts you on the Top leaderboard.

🟩 New Plot

Open up to 16 plots. The price rises with each plot.

✨ Super Soil

5 levels. Each one makes free-farm crops grow 10% faster. It does not shorten the 24-hour Nest crops.

🐦 Helper Bird

Up to 3 helpers. They auto-harvest ready crops.

⏩ Finish now

Skip the wait on one plot for 💎 0.2.

Claim or sell back

Claim your BIRD profit in the Nest, or sell gems back for BIRD at the live price. The fee is shown before you confirm.
